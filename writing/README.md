# Writing — Financial Services / Fintech

Content and research written from the vantage point of the language, localization,
and regulated-content layer of finance — the space **TransPerfect Financial
Services** occupies. The recurring thesis: **language and localization are
infrastructure in modern finance, and finance keeps treating them as an
afterthought.**

## The through-line

Interoperability debates stop at message formats. Digital-identity debates stop
at the credential. Agentic-commerce debates assume the human read the disclosure.
Inclusion debates skip the tens of millions who can't transact in their bank's
language. Every one of those conversations steps over the same layer — the one
this writing is about.
