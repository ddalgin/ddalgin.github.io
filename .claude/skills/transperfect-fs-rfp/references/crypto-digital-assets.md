# Crypto / Digital Assets Vertical (RFP intelligence)

Read this when an RFP is for a crypto exchange, wallet, or digital-asset firm (Binance.US, Coinbase,
Kraken, Crypto.com, Gemini, etc.). It gives the market context, the regulatory posture, and — most
importantly for localization bids — the **audience insight** that should shape the whole pitch.

## Bitcoin & crypto in one page

- **Origin:** Bitcoin introduced in a 2008 whitepaper by the pseudonymous **Satoshi Nakamoto**, launched
  Jan 2009 — a response to the financial crisis and distrust of banks. Decentralized, fixed supply of
  21M, "digital gold" / store-of-value thesis.
- **Cycles:** 2013–14 Mt. Gox era and collapse; 2017 bull run (~$20k) + ICO boom; 2018 bust; 2020–21
  institutional bull run (~$69k peak); 2022 "crypto winter" (Terra/Luna, Celsius, and the **FTX**
  collapse); 2023–24 recovery; **spot Bitcoin ETFs approved Jan 2024** (BlackRock et al.) brought
  mainstream/institutional money; 2024 halving; new all-time highs into 2025–26.
- **Landscape:** **Bitcoin** (store of value) remains #1; **Ethereum** #2 (smart contracts/DeFi);
  thousands of altcoins; **stablecoins** (USDT, USDC) are the workhorse for payments and remittances.
  Access is dominated by **centralized exchanges** — the vendors who buy localization.
- **Regulatory shift:** the US environment turned markedly **more crypto-friendly in 2025–26** — several
  SEC enforcement actions were dropped or settled, and firms that were under pressure began rebuilding.
  This matters: buyers are in expansion mode again, but they remain compliance-sensitive.

## Coinbase vs. Binance.US (US market)

**Coinbase**
- US-founded, **publicly traded (COIN, IPO 2021)**; the compliance-first, "most trusted" US brand.
- **US market leader — ~35% share (mid-2026)** `[VERIFY]`. Higher fees, strong institutional custody,
  regulatory goodwill.

**Binance (global)** vs **Binance.US**
- Binance is the world's largest exchange by volume, founded 2017 by **Changpeng "CZ" Zhao**. In **Nov
  2023** it settled with the US DOJ for **~$4.3B**; CZ pleaded guilty and stepped down.
- **Binance.US** is a separate US entity. 2023 regulatory/SEC action gutted it — it **lost USD banking
  rails and went crypto-only**, and its US share collapsed from ~20% to low single digits.
- **Comeback (2025–26):** restored **USD deposits/withdrawals (Feb 2025)**; the **SEC case was dismissed
  in 2025**; now (mid-2026) running an aggressive **near-zero-fee** strategy (0% maker) across 250+ pairs
  and **publicly targeting a return to ~20% US market share**, with plans to add derivatives, perps, and
  prediction markets pending licenses. `[VERIFY current numbers]`

**Why this matters for the RFP:** the Spanish-localization RFP is **not cosmetic — it's a growth lever in
an aggressive comeback**. Binance.US is fighting to reclaim share from an entrenched, compliant Coinbase.
Localization quality feeds directly into the two things they need most: **new-customer acquisition** and
**regulatory trust**. Frame TransPerfect's work as advancing both.

## The audience: US Hispanic / Latino crypto users (the heart of this bid)

The RFP asks for **US Spanish** and says the decision turns on regulated FS work in that variant. Here's
why that audience is the strategic prize:

- **They over-index on crypto ownership.** Multiple surveys put Hispanic crypto ownership above White
  Americans historically (e.g., ~16–17% vs ~11%; a Harris investor poll found **27% of Hispanic investors
  vs 17% of White**). A **Pew (Jan 2026)** read shows White adoption catching up (~18%), but Hispanic
  adoption remains high — this is a core crypto demographic, not a niche. `[VERIFY]`
- **Younger and mobile-first.** Latino millennials are reported **~90% more likely to invest in crypto**
  than the general population, and US Hispanics use fintech at the **highest rate of any group (~92%)**.
- **Real utility, not just speculation.** Heavy use for **remittances (US→Mexico/LatAm), cross-border
  payments, inflation hedging**, and as an on-ramp for those under-served by traditional banks.
- **Bottom line — yes, Latinos are a primary, high-growth, under-penetrated segment for a US exchange.**
  A US-Spanish push is a direct play to win them.

### What this means for the TransPerfect pitch
- **Authentic US Spanish is a conversion driver, not a checkbox.** This audience is bilingual and
  culturally discerning; neutral LatAm or Spain Spanish reads as inauthentic and erodes trust. In-country
  US-Hispanic linguists + a US-Spanish style guide and crypto glossary are the credibility play.
- **Segment the content, segment the workflow:**
  - *Marketing & lifecycle* → **transcreation** for a younger, mobile-first, culturally-specific audience;
    tone and cultural resonance matter more than literal fidelity.
  - *Legal & compliance / risk disclosures* → **accuracy-first, review-gated** translation; a firm
    rebuilding regulatory trust cannot afford mistranslated disclosures. Consider back-translation.
  - *Product strings (iOS/Android/web)* → **continuous localization** via GlobalLink connectors; fast,
    TM-driven, consistent terminology.
  - *Help center / support* → high-volume, agile; MTPE tolerable where quality allows.
- **Tie quality to their KPI.** Their goal is share reclaim; our message is that culturally-accurate US
  Spanish across product, marketing, compliance, and support **converts and retains the exact segment
  driving their comeback** — while keeping regulated content defensible.
- **Remittance/payments framing** resonates with this audience — reflect it where the content allows.
