## Service lines

### 1. Translation & Localization  ★ priority
The core offering: translation of documents, software, websites, apps, and content across 200+ languages.

- **Document translation** — reports, filings, contracts, marketing, disclosures, correspondence.
- **Software & app localization** — UI strings for iOS, Android, web; continuous/agile localization that
  plugs into CI/CD so product strings flow to translation and back automatically.
- **Website & digital localization** — via **GlobalLink** connectors (CMS, code repos, design tools).
- **Machine translation + post-editing (MTPE)** — human-in-the-loop MT for scale/speed where quality
  tolerance allows; custom-trained engines.
- **Linguistic assets** — translation memory (TM), termbases/glossaries, and style guides that enforce
  consistency and drive cost down over time (repetitions are discounted, not re-translated).

**GlobalLink** is TransPerfect's proprietary translation-management technology suite — the platform layer
(TMS/workflow, connectors, project management, MT, review portals, analytics). This is the crown jewel
for "platform + services" RFPs: TransPerfect owns both the platform and the service delivery, so it can be
sole prime with no third-party integration risk.

### 2. Interpretation & Multimedia  ★ priority
Spoken-language and media services.

- **Interpretation:** on-site, **over-the-phone (OPI)**, **video remote (VRI)**, and **remote
  simultaneous interpretation (RSI)** for meetings, earnings calls, AGMs, investor roadshows, board
  meetings, and customer support.
- **Multimedia localization:** subtitling, captioning, **voiceover and dubbing**, transcription, audio
  description, and full video localization.
- **E-learning & training localization:** localizing compliance, onboarding, and product-training
  courses (SCORM/LMS-ready) — high value for FS firms with global workforces and mandatory compliance
  training.

### 3. Global Marketing & Transcreation
Transcreation (creative adaptation, not literal translation) for campaigns, brand, and lifecycle content;
multicultural marketing; SEO/ASO localization; DTP/design localization. Relevant for FS brand and
customer-acquisition content where tone and persuasion matter more than word-for-word fidelity.

### 4. Legal Solutions (TLS)
eDiscovery, multilingual document review, deposition and litigation support, and legal translation.
Relevant to FS for regulatory investigations, cross-border disputes, and compliance/AML matters.

### 5. Life Sciences
Clinical-trial translation, regulatory submissions, linguistic validation, and the Trial Interactive
platform. (Adjacent to FS mainly for insurers/health-adjacent products.)

### 6. AI & Data Solutions (DataForce)
Data collection, annotation, and labeling for AI/ML training; model evaluation; multilingual datasets.
Relevant as FS firms build their own AI (chatbots, doc processing) and need training/eval data.

### 7. Staffing Solutions
Language-focused talent placement and managed staffing.

### Quality & language-QC capability (relevant to proofreading/QC RFPs)
Beyond translation, TransPerfect provides **monolingual and bilingual quality control, proofreading,
copyediting, and linguistic QA** — style-guide enforcement, terminology/consistency checks, formatting/DTP
QC across Word, Excel, and PowerPoint. This is the capability that answers proposal-QC RFPs (e.g., the
Fidelity engagement): editors enforce a client's style guide, check flow and completeness, and flag
errors like wrong client names carried over from prior bids.

1. **One accountable prime for platform + services** (GlobalLink + linguists) — no integration risk.
2. **Regulated-content expertise** — linguists and reviewers experienced in financial/regulatory material
   and the specific sub-vertical (asset management, banking, insurance, crypto/digital assets).
3. **Information security** — ISO 27001, NDAs, encryption, access controls, data residency `[VERIFY]`.
4. **Scale + speed** — largest linguist network means capacity for volume spikes and rush turnarounds
   without quality loss.
5. **Consistency engine** — TM, termbases, and style guides that keep terminology correct and compliant
   across every document and channel, and reduce cost over time.
6. **Global-local reach** — in-country linguists for locale accuracy (e.g., US Spanish ≠ neutral LatAm ≠
