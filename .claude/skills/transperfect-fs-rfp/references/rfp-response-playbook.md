## 1. The response method

1. **Read the whole RFP once for intent.** What broke? What are they really buying? Who evaluates it and
   how (is there a scoring rubric)?
2. **Extract a requirements checklist.** Every question, every mandatory deliverable, every eval
   criterion, every date, format, and word/page limit. Nothing gets answered until it's on the list.
3. **Confirm bid/no-bid & win themes.** Where do we genuinely fit? What are the 3–4 messages we want the
   evaluator to remember? (See win-theme library.)
4. **Draft answers to the buyer's pain**, not to a generic capability template. Reuse linguistic assets:
   consistent terminology, one voice.
5. **Insert `[placeholders]` for any client-specific commitment** (assigned team size, exact turnaround,
   price, named references) — these become contractual, so the bid owner must supply real values.
6. **QC pass:** completeness (every question answered), compliance (format/length/labels), consistency
   (client name correct everywhere — the classic error), and persuasion (does each answer close a gap?).

## 2. Standard FS RFP question bank + model answers

These are the questions FS localization/QC RFPs almost always ask. Adapt the model answers; fill
`[placeholders]`.

**Capacity / volume.** Frame to the client's stated volume and to the incumbent's failure if one exists.
> "TransPerfect is structured to support [client volume] comfortably. Our global network of 10,000+
> linguists `[VERIFY]` and [n] dedicated [language/service] specialists assigned to your account gives us
> baseline throughput of [X] plus elastic surge capacity for peaks and rush requests — so you are never
> gated by a single team's ceiling. A named primary team with trained backup coverage ensures continuity."

**Turnaround.** Give a standard SLA tier and note rush handling.
> "Standard turnaround is [24–48h] for [scope]; larger or complex work is scoped at intake with a
> committed date. Expedited and rush tiers are available (see pricing). On-time delivery is tracked and
> reported."

**Confidentiality / data security.** Lead with ISO 27001 and concrete controls.
> "All materials are handled under ISO 27001-certified `[VERIFY]` information-security management. Controls
> include NDAs with every linguist and project member, encryption in transit and at rest, role-based
> access limited to the assigned team, secure file transfer via [client platform or GlobalLink portal],
> and defined retention/secure-deletion policies. We will comply with your data-residency and any
> regulatory requirements."

**Scope of review / service depth** (for QC/proofreading).
> "Our review is multi-layered and configurable to your specification: style-guide enforcement
> (terminology, formatting, punctuation, tone); flow and clarity; completeness (confirming every question
> is answered); and consistency checks including client/entity names to catch wrong or stale references
> carried over from prior documents. We deliver tracked changes and queries so your team keeps control."

**Pricing.** Emphasize transparency and predictability — often the incumbent's failure.
> "Pricing is transparent and [per-word / per-page / per-project / tiered volume / retainer]. We provide
> itemized quotes at intake with no hidden fees, and translation-memory leverage means repeated content is
> discounted rather than re-billed. A retainer option supports predictable monthly budgeting."

**Rush charges.** Define "rush" precisely and price it upfront.
> "A rush request is any assignment requiring delivery in under [X hours] or same-business-day after
> [cutoff]. Rush work carries a [+25–50%] surcharge agreed before work begins — never applied
> retroactively. [Optionally: N rush jobs/month included under retainer.]"

**SLAs.** Say yes clearly.
> "Yes. We welcome an SLA covering turnaround, on-time delivery rate, quality/defect rate, capacity
> guarantees, and rush responsiveness, with agreed reporting cadence and remedies."

**Working hours / coverage.**
> "Standard coverage is [days/hours, aligned to the client's time zone]. Our follow-the-sun global network
> supports weekend, holiday, and after-hours coverage for time-sensitive work [included under / at the
> rush rate]."

**Technology / platform** (for localization).
> "GlobalLink, our proprietary translation-management suite, provides workflow automation, connectors to
> your CMS/repos/design tools, integrated MT, online review portals, and analytics/reporting. Because we
> own both the platform and the linguistic services, you contract with a single accountable prime."

**Quality methodology.**
> "Quality is engineered, not inspected in: qualified in-country linguists, TEP workflow (translate–edit–
> proofread), client-approved glossaries and style guides, and QA automation in GlobalLink. Linguistic
> validation and back-translation available where accuracy is regulatory-critical."

**References / case studies.** Only cite real, cleared references. `[VERIFY with bid team — never invent.]`

## 3. Win-theme library

Pick 3–4 per bid and thread them through every answer:

- **One prime, one accountable partner** — platform + services in-house; no integration/finger-pointing
  risk. (Strongest when the RFP contemplates separate platform and LSP vendors.)
- **Regulated-content confidence** — linguists and reviewers who know the sub-vertical and its rules, so
  translation/QC reduces the client's compliance exposure.
- **Security you can prove** — ISO 27001 `[VERIFY]` and concrete controls, not promises.
- **Scale without the ceiling** — largest network in the industry; capacity and rush without quality loss;
  directly cures an incumbent's capacity failure.
- **Consistency engine** — TM + termbase + style guide keep terminology correct across every asset and cut
  cost over time.
- **Locale precision** — true in-country variants (US Spanish ≠ neutral LatAm ≠ Spain).
- **Transparent, predictable pricing** — itemized, TM-leveraged, retainer options; cures pricing opacity.

## 4. Worked example A — Proposal QC / proofreading (Fidelity pattern)

*Situation:* FS asset manager needs QC/proofreading of its own RFP responses and sales bids — style-guide
enforcement plus flow/clarity/completeness/client-name checks. Volume 50–100 projects/month with rush
needs. Incumbent hit capacity limits and had opaque pricing. Formats: Word, Excel, PowerPoint.

*Win themes to lead with:* scale-without-the-ceiling (cures capacity failure), transparent pricing (cures
opacity), review depth beyond style guide (they explicitly asked), security.

*Framing moves:*
- Capacity answer must explicitly promise elastic surge + named backup so their timelines never slip
  again.
- Scope answer must confirm you do MORE than the style guide: flow, completeness ("did we answer every
  question"), and the wrong-client-name catch they specifically fear.
- Pricing answer must foreground transparency/predictability even if not cheapest.
- Note TransPerfect QC works natively in Word/Excel/PowerPoint with tracked changes.

## 5. Worked example B — US-Spanish localization, platform+services (Binance.US pattern)

*Situation:* Regulated crypto exchange running a competitive RFP for **US Spanish** localization across
product strings (iOS/Android/web), marketing & lifecycle content, legal/compliance documents, and help-
center content. **Model: one contract covering both the translation platform and the translation
services**; platform + LSP may bid jointly but one party must be prime. Shortlist gets a scored
translation test and volumes under NDA. Evaluation "decided" on regulated FS work in US Spanish.
Timeline: questions due Aug 17, proposals due Aug 28, intent-to-bid reply requested early.

*Why TransPerfect is uniquely strong here — lead with this:*
- **Sole prime, no joint bid.** TransPerfect provides BOTH the platform (GlobalLink) AND the linguistic
  services in-house. It directly satisfies "one contract covering both" as a single accountable prime — no
  JV, no platform-vs-LSP integration risk. This is the headline differentiator; open with it.

*Other win themes:* regulated-crypto/FS US-Spanish expertise (the stated deciding factor); continuous
localization for product strings via GlobalLink connectors to their repos/CI-CD; content-type-specific
workflows (transcreation for marketing, strict accuracy + legal review for compliance docs, fast agile
loops for help center); security/ISO 27001 and NDA-ready for the confidential test and volumes.

*Domain must-haves (see `fs-domain.md`):*
- **US Spanish specifically** — not neutral LatAm, not European Spanish. In-country US-Hispanic linguists;
  a US-Spanish style guide and financial/crypto glossary.
- **Regulated crypto content** — Binance.US operates under US money-transmitter/FinCEN and SEC scrutiny;
  legal/compliance strings need accuracy + client legal review, not creative liberty. Distinguish
  marketing (transcreation) from disclosures (precision + review).
- **The scored translation test** — flag that shortlisting hinges on it; recommend assigning top
  US-Spanish FS linguists and preparing a glossary/style guide before the test.
- **Content-type routing** — product strings (continuous, TM-driven), marketing/lifecycle (transcreation),
  legal/compliance (accuracy + review + possible back-translation), help center (agile, high-volume).

*Immediate actions for this pursuit:*
1. Send intent-to-bid before the reply deadline so TransPerfect is on the Q&A distribution.
2. Draft clarifying questions before the questions-due date (see fs-domain.md for a starter list:
   volumes, source formats, repo/CMS/TMS environment, existing TM/glossary/style guide, review cycles,
   test-content type, security/NDA requirements, incumbent situation, MT tolerance by content type).
3. Route to a linguist/PM with regulated US-Spanish FS experience — the stated deciding factor.
